fx is a friendly financial translator. Here's what it can understand.

## Numbers and currency talk

<Callout title="Try it">
  Every example below is a live, interactive widget — type in it.
</Callout>

**Numbers without a symbol** are implied to be your base currency:

<ExpressionPlayground preset="1000" small hideButtons />

**Numbers with a symbol** — include the currency symbol (`$`, `€`) before
the number (`$20.50`, `€10`), spaces allowed:

<ExpressionPlayground preset="$1000" small hideButtons />

**Numbers with a currency code** — a 3-letter code (`EUR`, `USD`, `EGP`)
before **or** after the number. fx even tolerates typos and stray
spacing, so `USD100`, `$ 100`, `100usd`, `100 uSd`, and `usD100` are all
the same:

<ExpressionPlayground preset="1000 usd" small hideButtons />

<Callout type="info" title="Grouping">
  Use parentheses `()` to group terms and control evaluation order, same
  as any calculator.
</Callout>

## Large numbers

Writing `11650000` out is hard to read. Use shorthand after a space:

- `k` — thousand (`1,000`)
- `m` — million (`1,000,000`)
- `b` — billion (`1,000,000,000`)
- `t` — trillion (`1,000,000,000,000`)

Or write them out verbally (`10 thousand`), or with commas
(`10,000`) — all three represent the same number:

<ExpressionPlayground preset="USD1k + 10 million usd + $11,650,000" small hideButtons />

## Supported operations

fx handles the arithmetic you'd expect:

- **Basic arithmetic**: `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`
- **Percentages**: expressed as multiplication — `15% * $200` is a 15%
  tip on $200
- **Exponents**: `^` — `2^3` is 2 multiplied by itself 3 times

<ExpressionPlayground preset="15% * $200" small hideButtons />

**Examples:**

- `$50 + €20` — mixes currencies
- `10k gbp + 5m usd` — uses abbreviations
- `20 thousand + 5 billion` — magnitude words
